About

Red & Green Stories are short 3D-animated bedtime stories starring two brothers, Red and Green. In every story they face a hard choice and learn something about kindness, courage, honesty and family.

The stories are made in Ghana by Richard Yaw Sewa for families everywhere. They're gentle, heartwarming and great to watch together before bed.

  • 💚 Kindness & forgiveness
  • ❤️ Brothers who stick together
  • 🌙 A new short story every day
