🐸 Privacy Policy

Red & Green Stories app and website

Last updated: 24 October 2026

For kids 🐸

Hi friend! This app lets you watch our frog stories. We never ask for your name, your photo, where you live or anything else about you. Sometimes you will see an ad. Ads are not part of our stories. If an ad asks you to tap or buy something, ask a grown-up first!

Who we are

Red & Green Stories (app ID com.redgreenstories.app) is made by Richard Yaw Sewa in Ghana. We make short animated moral stories for kids about kindness, courage, honesty and family. You can contact us at reghson1@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no sign-up, accounts, chat, comments or uploads. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Watching stories

When you tap a story, it opens in the YouTube app or your web browser. YouTube's own privacy policy and settings then apply: policies.google.com/privacy. We recommend parents use YouTube Kids or YouTube's supervised experiences for young children.

Ads (Google AdMob)

To keep our stories free, the app shows ads from Google AdMob, part of Google. We set every ad request as child-directed (age-restricted "child" treatment, following the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act and similar laws) and allow only ads rated G (suitable for general audiences, including families). This means:

  • Ads are not personalised. No interest-based ads and no remarketing.
  • The app does not use the Android Advertising ID. We removed that permission from the app.
  • Google may still process limited technical information (such as the device type, operating system, app version and IP address) to show an ad, prevent fraud and count ad views, as described in Google's policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

We only use Google Play's Families-certified ad network. You can learn more at AdMob and child-directed treatment.

Children's privacy

Our app is made for children and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the age required in your country). If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, please contact us and we will delete it.

Emails

If you email us, we only use your message to reply to you. We do not sell or share it.

Parents' tips

  • Please watch together with young children.
  • You can turn off internet access to stop ads and videos at any time.
  • On Android you can reset or delete the advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads. Our app does not use it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.
